PomoBlock

Stay focused. Block distractions.

PomoBlock is a simple, open-source Pomodoro timer designed to help you stay focused and boost your productivity. It blocks distracting websites and apps during your focus sessions, allowing you to work without interruptions.

Whether you're a student, professional, or anyone looking to improve their focus, PomoBlock is here to help you achieve your goals and make the most of your time.
